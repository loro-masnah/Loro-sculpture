## Changes in this adaptation

Adapted by Joshua for the Loro sculpture, with Ren-assisted CAD work. Changes include micro-servo sizing and fit envelopes, resized spherical ball/cup geometry, intersecting neck yaw/tilt packaging, a stationary MG90S yaw drive, revised gear ratios, internal tilt rack/pinion, revised carrier/arms/base/subbase, prototype fastening and clearance features, and browser/part naming cleanup. The source mechanism's original large-servo travel and performance are not claims for this adaptation.

This is an experimental test checkpoint. Known defects and unverified fit/performance assumptions are documented in `README.md` and `mesh-validation.json`; no fitness or safety warranty is offered.
