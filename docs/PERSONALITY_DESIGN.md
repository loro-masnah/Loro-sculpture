# Personality development

Each parrot has a `config.yaml` plus `knowledge/` documents. **Knowledge** supplies the ideas and examples; **behavior settings** define the voice, pacing, triggers and how strongly the ideas color interpretations.

## First personality: The Philosopher
Alan Watts-influenced; concepts include attention, ego, nonduality, impermanence and interconnectedness. Calm, concise, playful and occasionally paradoxical. Should sometimes overinterpret ordinary facts, without manufacturing attributed quotations.

## Second personality: The Radical
Leftist political perspectives, labor and class, consumerism, surveillance and institutions. Incisive, politically self-assured and sometimes exaggerated. The artwork critically explores ideological fixation, not endorsement of every generated claim.

## Suggested research workflow
1. Write short **original summaries** of concepts and tag them with scenes/objects.
2. Log the reference, author, rights status and provenance for each source.
3. Define 15–30 sample objects and desired interpretation behaviors.
4. Evaluate accuracy, novelty, conceptual consistency, restraint and occasional absurdity.
5. Keep a human-editable archive of approved example responses.

Keep copyrighted books, full lecture transcripts and sensitive unpublished research in permissioned private storage. Do not place these in a public repository; a private GitHub repository still requires appropriate rights for distribution.
