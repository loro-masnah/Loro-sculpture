# Artistic concept — AI Parrot
A parrot made from discarded materials observes people and objects, then repeats what its knowledge and conditioning tell it to say. It is a metaphor for humans consuming, internalizing, and amplifying ideas in social media, philosophical traditions, and political movements. The same reality yields radically different interpretations.

The sculptural material is part of the meaning: recycled matter becomes a body that recycles received ideas.

## Core mechanism
Observe → describe factual scene → retrieve worldview-specific material → generate short interpretation → speak → wait.

The first one-parrot prototype uses a contemplative personality influenced by Alan Watts and Eastern philosophy. A second, politically radical perspective is documented but not part of the first finished sculpture. The architecture should expand to five parrots in a later phase.

A strong work may sound insightful sometimes and mechanical, dogmatic, or absurd at other times. Never misattribute generated dialogue to a historical thinker.
