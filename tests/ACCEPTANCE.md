# Acceptance checklist
## Hardware
- [ ] Pi RAM, OS image and power supply recorded
- [ ] BrainCraft HAT is mounted and recognized
- [ ] Camera appears in `rpicam-hello --list-cameras`
- [ ] Camera takes sharp test still
- [ ] Enclosed speaker outputs intelligible audio
- [ ] No undervoltage or overheating

## Software and persona
- [ ] Describes at least 10 everyday scenes accurately
- [ ] Retrieves relevant Philosopher concept notes
- [ ] Produces concise, original spoken interpretation
- [ ] Never fabricates Alan Watts quotations
- [ ] Cooldown avoids repeated or overlapping phrases
- [ ] Restarts after reboot; gracefully handles network/API errors
- [ ] Does not persist visitor images by default

## Physical integration
- [ ] Speech audible at 1–2 meters in expected venue
- [ ] Camera view aligned to intended interaction distance
- [ ] Power, airflow, mounting and access safe
- [ ] 2–4 hour continuous test completed
- [ ] Artist signs off on final voice/personality
